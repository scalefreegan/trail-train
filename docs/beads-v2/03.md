## What
B-race (tune-up) schema and server. race.json gains `kind: "a"|"b"` (default a) and `parent_slug` for b; validateRaceJson enforces (b requires an existing parent A folder; b needs no nutrition/plan). `POST /api/races` quick-create: {name, date, distance_mi, gain_ft, timezone?, parent_slug, gpx?} → slug derived, folder written with agent-free provenance by "user", optional GPX upload → course build. race-payload adds `b_races[]` (slug, name, date, distance_mi, gain_ft, weeks_out) for the training A-race; facts.mjs adds `race.b_races[]`; coach-prompt's race paragraph tells the coach to plan taper and recovery around each. listRaces/switcher payload groups B under A.

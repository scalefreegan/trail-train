## What
Acclimation inputs and altitude calibration. profile.physiology gains `home_elevation_ft` (settings section + PHYSIOLOGY_FIELDS bounds 0–12,000); `scripts/acclimation.mjs` derives `arrival_at_altitude` from the calendar sync's classified travel events (last travel event before race day whose title/location matches the race location words, else the day before the race) with the source reported; the planner shows acclimation days + source with a manual override persisted per slug. calibration.ts back-test gains an altitude bias: activities whose mean elevation (from cached streams under .cache/strava-streams or climbs.json) is ≥ 2,000 ft above home compare predicted vs actual; with ≥ 5 such runs it suggests altitude_pct, else reports "uncalibrated".
## Acceptance Criteria
- scripts/acclimation.test.mjs: derivation from fixture calendar events (match, no match → day-before default, override wins).
- calibration: unit test with synthetic activities proving the bias and the ≥5 gate; ModelCheck shows the suggestion or "uncalibrated" honestly.
- Settings PUT accepts home_elevation_ft; profile.example.json documents it.

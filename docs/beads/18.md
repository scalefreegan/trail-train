## What
Phone-friendly race-day mode at `/race-day` (a hash route inside the Vite app, no router dependency): current race-local time, elapsed since start, next station with best/expected/worst ETA and cutoff margin (from the shared projection), what to pick up there (fuel plan leg), crew drive ETA if `features.crew` and `crew.private.json` exists, and the following two stations. Large type, single column, works at 360 px wide, survives offline reload via the browser cache of the last payload (no service worker required in v1; note the limitation). Reached from the switcher menu on an active race whose date is today ± 1 day.

## Why
The printed cards are the source of truth on course; this is the glanceable companion the interview added to scope.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Renders from `useRacePlan()` inside `RacePlanProvider` with no new data fetching beyond what the race view needs.
- Accessible from a phone on the LAN (`vite --host`; document the one-line change in README) with the dev server's Origin check allowing the LAN origin explicitly (config key, not a blanket allow).
- `npm run build` passes; a screenshot at 360 px in the closing note.

## Technical Context
- Projection helpers: `web/src/race/pacing.ts` `elapsedAtMile`, `mileAtElapsed`, `fmtRaceClock`; fuel legs from `nutrition.ts` `planFuel`.
- Clock helper from the timezone bead.

## Constraints
- No router or PWA libraries.

## Out of Scope
Live GPS position; push notifications.

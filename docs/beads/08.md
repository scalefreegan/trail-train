## What
Gate the race and nutrition views by `race.json.features` and `visual.panels`. Crew column, crew sheet button, crew-stop slider and crew ETAs exist only when `features.crew`; drop-bag card and drop-bag rows only when `features.drop_bags`; pacer flags when `features.pacers`; night bands and caffeine schedule when `features.night`; heat bands and `fluid_ml_hr_heat` when `features.heat`; altitude caveat on projections when `features.altitude`; `visual.panels.climb_comparison|model_check|crew_sheet` hide those panels. A crewless 50k renders planner + runner card + fuel card only.

## Why
The schema supports 50k–100mi trail ultras; the views must not show a crew sheet for a race that forbids crew.

## Acceptance Criteria
- `web/src/race/RacePlanner.tsx`: station table columns and the four card buttons (~lines 8-11 imports; buttons in the panel header) conditional on features/panels; sliders for aid/crew stop minutes conditional.
- `NutritionPlan.tsx`: caffeine section only with `night`; heat rows only with `heat`; drop-bag packing list only with `drop_bags`; altitude paragraph rendered from `coach_notes.altitude` when `features.altitude`.
- `ModelCheck.tsx` shows an "altitude not modeled" caveat line when `features.altitude` (PRD: no pacing-math change).
- Fixture-based render test is not required (no test runner in web); instead add `web/src/race/features.ts` with pure `visiblePanels(race)` and `visibleColumns(race)` functions and cover them with `node --test` after compiling via `tsc -b` (or duplicate the tiny logic in `scripts/` — pick one, document it).
- A synthetic `races/_fixtures/crewless-50k/` folder (committed, no personal data) used to eyeball the gated render; excluded from `listRaces` by the underscore prefix.

## Technical Context
- Types: `web/src/race/types.ts`; plan hook `useRacePlan.ts`; fuel model `nutrition.ts` `planFuel` (drop-bag assignment) and `caffeine.ts`.

## Constraints
- Feature flags default to `true` when absent (old folders keep working).

## Out of Scope
Theme, hero image, panel reordering.

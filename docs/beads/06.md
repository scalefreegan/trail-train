## What
Make all race clocks race-local. `race.json.timezone` (IANA) drives: the countdown and "race day" chips, `useBlockConfig().date` construction (today `new Date(\`${date}T${start_time}:00\`)` at `web/src/data.ts` ~435 uses the browser zone), night intervals in `web/src/race/pacing.ts` `nightIntervals`, heat bands in `caffeine.ts`, crew ETAs in `CrewSheet.tsx`/`RunnerCard.tsx`, and the race-week protocol in `web/src/race/NutritionPlan.tsx` whose weekday names ("wed → the gun", "Friday", "Saturday", ~546-610) are computed from `date` + `timezone` instead of hard-coded. Sunrise/sunset are computed in `build-course.mjs` from the start coordinates, date, and timezone (NOAA solar algorithm, no dependency) and written to `race.json.sun` with provenance `by: "computed"`.

## Why
San Juan Softie starts Friday 6:00 AM Mountain; MM100 was Saturday in Arizona (no DST). Today every clock assumes the laptop's zone and a Saturday start.

## Acceptance Criteria
- A helper `web/src/race/clock.ts` exporting `raceStart(race): Date`, `raceLocalParts(date, tz)`, `weekdayName(date, tz)` implemented with `Intl.DateTimeFormat` (no date library).
- Countdown is correct when the browser zone differs from the race zone (test with `TZ=Pacific/Auckland npm run build && vitest`-free check: a `node --test` for the pure helper duplicated in `scripts/clock.mjs` and imported by both sides, or the TS helper compiled by tsc into the test).
- NutritionPlan race-week prose reads: "<D-3 weekday> to the gun", "<D-2> and <D-1>", "nothing after noon <D-1>", etc., verified for a Friday and a Saturday race in a snapshot test of the generated strings.
- `sun` computed for MM100 matches the hand-authored 18:35 / 06:15 within 5 minutes.
- Pacing localStorage keys (`race.goal_h`, `race.fatigue_pct_v2`, ...) in `web/src/race/useRacePlan.ts` are namespaced by slug (`race.<slug>.goal_h`); the default `goal_h` is derived from cutoff × 0.85 rounded, not 32.

## Technical Context
- `useRacePlan.ts` localStorage knob list (~lines 20-60); `pacing.ts` `nightIntervals(startClock, sunset, sunrise, horizonH)`; `caffeine.ts` `sunBounds`/`heatBands`.
- `scripts/build-course.mjs` is where course-derived race.json fields are written back.

## Constraints
- No date libraries. Keep imperial internal units.

## Out of Scope
Altitude in the pacing model. Theme.
